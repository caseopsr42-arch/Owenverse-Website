What this site collects

The Owenverse website does not directly collect names, email addresses, payment information, or other personal information. Embedded YouTube video and linked Google Forms may collect information under their own privacy policies.

How information is used

Information submitted through the guest application is received and handled through Google Forms by the show operator. The website itself does not process or store those submissions.

Third-party services

YouTube may set cookies or receive technical information when an embedded video is loaded. The guest application opens Google Forms in a separate service. Social links open third-party platforms. Review those providers' privacy policies for their practices.

Your choices

You can dismiss the site's informational notice, avoid playing the embedded video, or choose not to use the guest application and social links. Browser controls can also limit cookies.

Contact and review

Use the contact email in the footer for questions about this site. The final policy, retention practices, rights notices, and any jurisdiction-specific language should be completed with an attorney before launch.
